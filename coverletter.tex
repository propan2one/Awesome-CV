\begin{cvletter}

Over the past five years, I have seen bioinformatics establish itself as an indispensable pillar within the laboratory teams. I have observed this same growing enthusiasm for transforming complex data workflow into robust production bioinformatics pipelines, deployed on an industrial scale to support vaccine quality control and safety. Building on this dynamic, my ambition has always been clear: to make Sanofi’s bioinformatics team a global leader in scientific innovation and technological excellence. It is with this commitment that I'm pleased to apply for the position of Manager of Head of Advanced Bioinformatics \& NGS Analytic within Sanofi Vaccines R\&D and the Data \& Computational Science (DCS) department .

\lettersection{About Me}
My expertise is rooted in my multidisciplinary background, which combines laboratory work and bioinformatics. I began my career as an immunologist before making a radical shift toward bioinformatics during my Ph.D. This dual scientific background has become my greatest asset: having conducted experiments myself on a daily basis, I have an insider’s understanding of the needs, constraints, and challenges faced by biologists and lab teams who use computational tools. I know what it’s like to deal with a challenging sample, a protocol that could be improved, or a biological question that isn’t properly formalized in terms of data. It is this understanding that allows me to design truly useful, end-user-focused analyses and pipelines.

This practical experience has also shaped my approach to science: I'm curious by nature, and that is what drives me to start projects and build connections between people with different areas of expertise. Where others see disciplinary boundaries, I see opportunities for collaboration. I enjoy identifying needs before they are even formulated, bringing the right people together around a shared vision, and working with them to build solutions that exceed the sum of individual contributions. It is this same enthusiasm that led me to found and develop the Vaccine Bioinformatics Community, a community of practice designed to break down silos, connect expertise scattered across teams and sites, and foster common standards for data pipelines and governance.

\lettersection{Why Me?}
Having worked closely with a large part of the team, I'm familiar with each person’s strengths. This knowledge will allow me to streamline coordination, create synergies among bioinformaticians, and maximize collective performance from day one. At the same time, I also have a strong connection to the field. My past experiences working with many of our end users across the various MLE/TOR/WAL sites have given me an understanding of their real needs, which will help ensure business continuity. Finally, over time, we have developed the fundamental building blocks that will allow us to go much further, particularly in the area of agent-based AI models: taking on this role today means ensuring a smooth technical transition and immediately capitalizing on an architecture designed for the future, at the very moment when Sanofi is placing agent-based and AI-driven workflows at the heart of its bioinformatics strategy.
By combining this technical expertise, user empathy, and in-depth knowledge of the internal ecosystem, I will be operational and able to generate value from the moment I arrive, without the need for a lengthy onboarding process.
